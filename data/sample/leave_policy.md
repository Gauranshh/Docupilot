# Northwind Labs — Leave Policy

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: People Operations

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Labs Pvt. Ltd. in India. Interns and
contractors follow the separate Contractor Engagement Guidelines.

## 2. Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave for employees who join mid-year is
credited on a pro-rata basis, rounded to the nearest half day.

## 3. Types of leave

### 3.1 Earned leave (EL)
- Every full-time employee earns 18 days of earned leave per leave year, credited at 1.5 days per month.
- Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance for absences of 3 days or more.
- Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Anything above 30 days lapses on 31 December.
- On resignation, unused earned leave (up to the 30-day cap) is encashed at basic salary.

### 3.2 Casual leave (CL)
- 8 days of casual leave per leave year, credited in full on 1 January.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward and is not encashable.
- No more than 3 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at once.

### 3.3 Sick leave (SL)
- 10 days of paid sick leave per leave year.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave can accumulate up to a maximum of 45 days.

### 3.4 Parental leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Paternity leave: 15 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Adoption leave: 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver of a child under 3 months old.

### 3.5 Bereavement leave
Up to 5 working days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child,
parent, sibling, parent-in-law).

### 3.6 Public holidays
Northwind Labs observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published by People Operations
every December. Employees may swap up to 2 listed holidays for regional festivals of their choice.

## 4. Applying for leave
All leave is applied for through the HR portal (hr.northwind.internal). Your reporting manager approves
leave within 2 working days. Unapproved absence for more than 3 consecutive working days is treated
as unauthorised absence and may lead to loss of pay.

## 5. Loss of pay
Leave taken beyond available balances is treated as leave without pay (LWP) and is deducted at
1/30th of monthly gross salary per day.
