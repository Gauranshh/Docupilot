# Northwind Labs — Remote Work & IT Security Guidelines

Version 1.4 · Effective 1 June 2026 · Owners: People Operations and Information Security

## 1. Hybrid work model
Northwind Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays
("anchor days"). Teams may choose one additional common office day. Fully remote arrangements need
approval from the department head and People Operations, and are reviewed every 6 months.

## 2. Working from another city or country
- Working from another city in India for up to 30 days per year is allowed with manager approval.
- Working from outside India is limited to 15 working days per calendar year and needs approval from
  People Operations at least 21 days in advance, because of tax and data-protection obligations.
- Employees may not work from countries on the company's restricted list (published by Legal).

## 3. Home-office allowance
New employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of ₹15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor.
An internet reimbursement of ₹1,500 per month is paid with salary to all hybrid and remote employees.

## 4. Company devices
- Only company-issued laptops may be used to access source code, customer data, or internal systems.
- Laptops must have full-disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent, and automatic OS updates enabled. IT checks compliance daily; non-compliant devices lose VPN access.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to it-security@northwind.example within 2 hours.
- Devices are refreshed every 3 years. Engineers may request a refresh after 2 years.

## 5. Accounts and passwords
- Single sign-on (SSO) with multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company applications.
- Use the company password manager for any credential not covered by SSO. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
- Never share credentials, including with IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## 6. Data handling
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always Restricted:
it must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage, or pasted into AI tools that are
not on the approved-tools list. Approved AI tools are listed on the IT portal.

## 7. Public Wi-Fi
When working from cafés, airports or hotels, always connect through the company VPN. Do not take
video calls that discuss Restricted data in public spaces.

## 8. Reporting incidents
Report suspected phishing using the "Report phish" button in the mail client. Report any other
security incident to it-security@northwind.example or the #security-help Slack channel immediately.
