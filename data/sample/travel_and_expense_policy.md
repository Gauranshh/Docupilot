# Northwind Labs — Travel & Expense Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Finance

## 1. Principles
Spend company money as you would your own. All business travel must be pre-approved by your
reporting manager in the travel portal before booking. Bookings made without pre-approval are not
reimbursed unless the trip was an emergency approved in writing within 24 hours.

## 2. Air and rail travel
- Domestic flights: economy class for all employees. Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- International flights: economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires CFO approval.
- Rail: AC 2-tier or AC chair car. AC first class is allowed for Director level and above.

## 3. Hotel limits (per night, including taxes)
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): up to ₹7,500.
- Other cities in India: up to ₹4,500.
- International: up to USD 180, or USD 250 in high-cost cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Singapore, Tokyo, Zurich).
Stays above these limits need pre-approval from your department head.

## 4. Daily allowance (meals and incidentals)
- Domestic travel: ₹1,200 per day. No receipts required.
- International travel: USD 60 per day. No receipts required.
Daily allowance is not paid for days when meals are provided by the hotel package or event organiser.

## 5. Local conveyance
App-based cabs (standard category) are reimbursed at actuals. Premium cab categories are not reimbursed
unless no standard cab is available, which must be noted in the claim. Use of a personal car for business
is reimbursed at ₹12 per km; two-wheelers at ₹5 per km.

## 6. Client entertainment
Client meals are reimbursed up to ₹3,000 per attendee, including the employee. The names and company of
all attendees must be listed in the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client entertainment events.

## 7. Submitting claims
- Submit expense claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
- Attach itemised receipts for every expense above ₹500, except daily allowance.
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle if approved before the 20th of the month.

## 8. Non-reimbursable items
Personal entertainment, minibar charges, laundry for trips shorter than 5 nights, traffic fines,
upgrades not covered above, and loyalty-programme fees are not reimbursed.
